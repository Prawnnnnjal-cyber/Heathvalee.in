UNDERSTANDING EXTREME HEAT

What is a Heatwave?

Understanding heat risk helps communities prepare for extreme temperature conditions.

01

Extreme Temperature

Heatwaves involve unusually high temperatures that can affect people, infrastructure and ecosystems.

02

Urban Heat

Dense buildings, roads and limited vegetation can make cities retain more heat.

03

Health Risk

Prolonged exposure to extreme heat can increase heat-related risks, especially for vulnerable populations.

PRACTICAL ACTIONS

How to Stay Safe During Extreme Heat

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink water regularly and avoid dehydration during hot weather.

🏠

Stay Cool

Use shaded or cool spaces and reduce unnecessary exposure to direct sunlight.

🧢

Protect Yourself

Wear lightweight clothing and use appropriate protection when outdoors.

📱

Monitor Conditions

Follow reliable weather and air-quality information before planning outdoor activities.
